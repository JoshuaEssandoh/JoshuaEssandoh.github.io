 I'm a Computer Engineering student at Virginia Tech (Class of 2027) with a strong foundation in machine learning, software development, and research. He has contributed to published research in machine learning applications, including oyster identification using CNNs and YOLO, and educational technology interventions, with work presented at IEEE Big Data and ACM SIGCHI conferences.
I have gained practical experience through internships at Boeing, where I containerized applications using Docker, and at Salisbury University's NSF REU program, where I developed ML-powered detection systems and data-driven web applications. I currently conducts undergraduate research at Virginia Tech's Hume Center, applying machine learning to detect anomalies in wireless communications.
As External Affairs Chair and former Regional Leadership Chair for the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE), I lead programming and professional development for 200+ members across multiple chapters. My technical expertise spans Python, JavaScript, TensorFlow, PyTorch, cloud platforms, and full-stack development—skills he recently showcased by winning first place at VTHacks 13 with a voice-powered reading assistant for visually impaired users.
